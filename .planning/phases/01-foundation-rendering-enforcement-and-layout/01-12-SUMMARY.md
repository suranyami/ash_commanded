## Accomplishments

- Replaced `Enum.reject(actions_used, &(&1 in [:create, :update, :destroy, :read]))` with
  `Enum.reject(actions_used, fn action -> action in @builtin_actions end)`. The four atoms are
  unchanged and the same actions are still rejected.
- Hoisted the four standard verbs into `@builtin_actions`, so the list is named once and the
  rejection reads as one concept.
- Recorded the reason in place: Elixir 1.20 + OTP 27 mis-expands `in` against a literal list
  longer than three entries inside a short-hand anonymous function, `:cover` re-compilation then
  raises `MatchError :error` (elixir#15546 / erlang/otp#11524), and the workaround may be removed
  once the OTP floor is 28 or newer.
- Confirmed the rewrite is transformer-side only: no golden file changed, and the golden render
  and budget suites pass unchanged.
- Ran the local gate: `mix check` exited 0 (format, compile with warnings as errors, credo with
  no issues, dialyzer passed, docs), and `MIX_ENV=test mix coveralls` exited 0 with **277 tests,
  0 failures** and 77.6% total coverage.
